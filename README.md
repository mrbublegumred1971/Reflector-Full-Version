<a id="overview"></a>

**Reflector** by Squirrels, LLC. is a powerful mirroring application designed for Windows that allows you to share and display content from your mobile devices directly onto your computer screen. With the **latest version**, you can enjoy a seamless experience of viewing your smartphone or tablet's content on a larger display, making it perfect for presentations, gaming, and media sharing. 

Reflector supports multiple streaming protocols including Google Cast, AirPlay, and AirParrot, ensuring compatibility with a wide range of devices.
